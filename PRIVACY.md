# Privacy Policy: NF Modal Remover

NF Modal Remover does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The list of websites you choose is saved only in Safari's local storage for the extension, on your own device.
- The extension reads and changes pages only on the websites you add, and only to hide pop-up elements.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

If you have questions, open an issue on this project's GitHub page.
