Privacy policy

NF Modal Remover does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Last updated 8 November 2026

What the app stores

The only thing NF Modal Remover saves is the list of websites you add. That list stays in Safari's local storage for the extension, on your own Mac. It is never sent anywhere.

What the extension can see

The extension runs only on the websites in your list. On those sites, it looks for page elements with the CSS class nf-modal, removes them, and turns page scrolling back on. It doesn't read, record or send the content of any page.

On every other website, the extension does not run at all.

What the app doesn't do

  • No network requests
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No sharing of data with anyone, including the developer

Removing your data

Remove sites from the list in the extension's popup, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
